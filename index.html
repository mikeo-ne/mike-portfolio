01 — About

The producer who learned the room first.

Before I ever sold a beat or ran a vocal session, I spent five years inside Kampala's live-music ecosystem — managing dancers, booking deejays, and running small-scale events. That background shaped everything I do now. I know what a speaker stack feels like from the floor. I know which frequencies make a room lose its mind.

Today I split my time between three lanes: engineering and producing records for a growing roster of Ugandan talent, helming producer-led singles and collaborative projects (most recently the Love Look What You Made Me Do EP with Kai & River), and running 7H Music Group — an independent label and creative company handling A&R, executive production, and release rollouts for artists ready to be heard beyond the 256.

I work primarily in Afrobeats, Hip Hop, R&B, Amapiano, and Reggae. I believe records should feel inevitable — like they couldn't have been made any other way.
